## 🌍 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Knowrex AI is an open-source, AI-powered customer support system that directly contributes to **7 UN Sustainable Development Goals**. By providing free, accessible, and efficient knowledge management technology, Knowrex helps organizations of all sizes deliver better service while promoting education, economic growth, innovation, equality, and responsible resource consumption.

**Primary SDG Alignment:** SDG 8, SDG 9, SDG 10
